Crypto Card Launch

Research to guide the launch of a crypto card.

Research Design

Stage 1: 16 user interviews and a survey of 1,339 users across 32 countries. Stage 2: a follow-up survey of 462 users.

Key Findings

Identified user groups, spending needs and barriers to adoption. Ease of use, clear fees and reliable payments shaped users’ choice of card.

Recommendations & Product Implications

Research shaped target audiences, product positioning and launch priorities. Recommended simpler payments, transparent pricing and features for everyday spending.

Walt · Monthly surveys and feedback tracking

High-Volume Trader Feedback

Monthly research to improve the experience of high-volume traders.

Research Design

Ran monthly surveys and built a Notion dashboard to track CSAT, recurring issues and team actions. The team used it to prioritise fixes and follow up on feedback.

Key Findings

Traders needed more control over orders and open positions, easier chart navigation and quick access to favourite assets.

Recommendations & Product Implications

Research led to shipped improvements: limit orders, partial position closing, adding to open positions, asset watchlists, flexible stop-loss controls, and chart zoom and scrolling. Fixed issues stopped recurring in later feedback.

Research Repository and AI Assistants

Made existing research easier for all teams to find and use.

Research Design

Organised the research repository and built AI research assistants that help teams find relevant studies, summarise findings and answer product questions using existing research. Instructions required source citations, dates and clear evidence to reduce unsupported answers and make gaps explicit.

Key Findings

Example use cases: helped the Chief Product Officer understand why users left and what still needed fixing; helped a product owner improve welcome offers using findings from earlier campaigns.

Recommendations & Product Implications

Teams found answers much faster and avoided repeat studies. The welcome-offer recommendations were tested and implemented.

Walt · Interviews, survey and desk research

Loyalty Program Research

Research to understand what makes a loyalty programme valuable and engaging, and guide improvements to the existing programme.

Research Design

Interviewed 8 highly engaged users, surveyed 1,342 participants in the following season, and compared 22 loyalty programmes and reward mechanics alongside 6 academic studies.

Key Findings

Satisfaction was linked to teamwork, competition and trust. Dissatisfied users wanted fairer opportunities for participants with smaller balances and rewards that matched expectations.

Recommendations & Product Implications

Research clarified what users valued and what discouraged participation. Findings led to short reward challenges and informed plans for new ranking formats. Recommended clearer point values, guaranteed rewards and more ways to earn and redeem points.
